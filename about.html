Founder

Kishore Iyer

ACA · ACWA · ACS

A Chartered Accountant with close to four decades of experience across leading financial and corporate institutions, Kishore Iyer brings extensive expertise in financial services, business management, governance and organisational leadership; he began his career with Reliance Industries, followed by over a decade with DSP Merrill Lynch, before joining Nomura, where he held senior leadership responsibilities including Interim Country Head and Chief Operating Officer of Nomura India

His career has given him deep experience in financial management, business strategy, governance, risk management, regulatory and compliance matters, operational effectiveness and organisational leadership; he has worked extensively with senior management, boards, regulators and global stakeholders, navigating complex business environments and helping organisations build strong and sustainable operating frameworks

At Vellcrest, Kishore combines this extensive corporate experience with a hands-on, solutions-oriented approach to help businesses strengthen their financial foundations, governance, processes and decision-making capabilities
